LPC

Track late payment charges per loan. Enter records by hand below, or upload an Excel file the same way as Loan chart — saved on this device. Whatever you type for loan amount and LPC %, the LPC amount is calculated for you (loan amount × LPC%, rounded to the nearest rupee) — a sheet's own LPC-amount column, if any, is ignored so it can't drift out of sync with the two you actually entered.

Customer search

Searches whatever's loaded in the Loan chart tab — upload your loan book there once and it's shared here automatically, saved on this device. Search by name, A/C no., or phone number; mark Paid/Unpaid or Active/Deactivated right here and it updates the same record everywhere else in the tool.

Paid tracker — by loan title

Every customer from the Loan chart, split Paid vs Unpaid within each product — for a month-end check of who's paid and who hasn't. Tick unpaid customers and send an EMI reminder straight from here.
